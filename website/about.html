Who Are We

About Encik Beku

Encik Beku is an established service company in Malaysia, specializing in air conditioning services with a strong presence across Kuala Lumpur / Shah Alam (HQ), Negeri Sembilan, Johor Bahru, and Penang. The company provides complete aircond solutions, including installation, repair, maintenance, and supply for residential and commercial clients.

In addition to air conditioning, Encik Beku also offers various technical and renovation services under dedicated sub-brands, creating a convenient one-stop solution for homes and businesses. Supported by dedicated service websites and digital marketing, Encik Beku continues to grow as a fast, reliable, and affordable service provider in Malaysia.

To become a leading one-stop air conditioning and technical service provider in Malaysia, recognized for fast response, reliable service, and practical solutions for homes and businesses.

To deliver affordable and high-quality services supported by skilled workmanship, professional service standards, and a smooth customer experience across every project and touchpoint.

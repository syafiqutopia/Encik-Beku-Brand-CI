Clear space

Keep clear space equal to 1× the cap-height of the wordmark on all four sides. No text, icon, graphic, image, border or other element may enter that area.

Logo misuse

Eight things that are never allowed

Misuse weakens recognition, reduces legibility and creates inconsistency across branded materials.

1
Do not use unapproved colours in the logo
2
Do not place the logo on a background that reduces legibility
3
Do not change the typeface, or recreate or manipulate the wordmark or icon
4
Do not crop or remove any part of the logo
5
Do not outline or create a keyline around the logo
6
Do not rotate the logo
7
Do not distort, warp or stretch the logo
8
Do not add a drop shadow or any other effect
Colour palette

Blue for cooling, one orange for action

Blue tones represent cooling, reliability and performance. Pumpkin Spice is used strategically for highlights, important information and selected calls to action. Keep to roughly 60% primary / 30% secondary / 10% accent, with neutrals as required for readability.

Three colours cannot carry text

Measured against white: Fresh Sky 2.44:1, Pumpkin Spice 2.65:1, Frozen Lake 1.69:1 — all below the 4.5:1 minimum for body text. Use them as fills and graphics only. For orange text on a light background use #B84E06 (5.08:1).

Orange fills take near-black text

White on Pumpkin Spice measures 2.65:1 and fails at every size; near-black #0B0E12 on Pumpkin Spice is 7.30:1. This looks unusual beside a white-on-blue button. It is correct — do not change it to white.

Typography

Satoshi, three weights, nothing else

Satoshi was selected because it is modern, clean, geometric, highly readable and works equally well in print and digital. There is no second family.

Tone of voice

Short, practical, friendly

For Malaysian audiences, use straightforward Malay or simple English rather than complicated language. Confident, without sounding overly corporate.

Say it like this

  • “Chemical wash for a 1.0HP wall unit — RM180 per unit.”
  • “Installation includes 6 ft of copper piping.”
  • “12 months workmanship warranty.”

Not like this

  • “Comprehensive maintenance solutions tailored to your requirements.”
  • “Industry-leading quality you can trust.”
  • “Hurry — limited time offer, book now!”
Visual direction

Clean, technical, cooling-oriented

Aim for

  • Generous white space and a strong grid
  • Strong blue sections, dark-blue headings, light-blue surfaces
  • Orange used selectively, never as a background wash
  • Real project and real technician photography
  • Consistent iconography and typography

Avoid

  • Overly decorative layouts and competing colours
  • Excessive text density and visual clutter
  • Poor contrast and inconsistent icon sets
  • Generic stock photography
  • Countdown timers, discount banners, fake urgency

Tinting Pumpkin Spice over a blue field turns it muddy grey and reads as a printing fault. Motifs on blue should use Frozen Lake or white; orange on blue only at full opacity in a solid shape.
